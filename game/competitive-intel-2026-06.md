# CHROMA Competitive Intel — June 2026 wave

Source: live web research, 2026-06-14. Last wave: 2026-05-24.

## Bottom line

**The category shifted under us this wave.** Block Blast is still #1 but
sharing oxygen with a new **sort-puzzle sub-genre** (Arrows Puzzle
Escape, Arrows GO!, Marble Sort, Bus Rush Fever) that broke into
Sensor Tower's April 2026 top-10 simultaneously. **Color Block Jam**
(Rollic) is doing **~$12M/mo IAP+IAA** on color-as-target mechanics.

**Hungry Studio shipped no headline features in the last 6 weeks** —
changelogs read "bug fixes," version cadence is weekly through 7.1.0
with no mode launches. They are in pure optimization mode. **The
color-mechanic window is open.** It probably closes within 2 quarters
of Block Blast seeing this work elsewhere — historically they absorb
adjacent mechanics fast.

**App Store editorial in May 2026 favored narrative/cozy/themed
puzzles** (Phozzle, TR-49, Is This Seat Taken?, Blue Prince in the ADA
finalists). Not block-puzzle spectacle, not pure minimalism. The
implication: CHROMA's slot-1 should lean on the theme + brand
personality, not pure abstract.

## What's new in the category

- **Sort-puzzle wave is the threat to watch.** Arrows GO! ($X / mo,
  top-10 downloads), Marble Sort, Bus Rush Fever — all single-mechanic
  sub-30-sec sessions, tap-the-clear-arrow / sort-by-color. **They are
  not Block Blast clones** — they are a parallel attack on the same
  casual-puzzle audience using simpler mechanics and color-as-target.
- **Color Block Jam (Rollic)** is the closest direct revenue comp at
  ~800K downloads/mo, ~$6M/mo (March 2026 Apptopia data). Three-stage
  monetization funnel: 50 free levels → banner + IAP prompts at
  difficulty spikes → full interstitials. Free + paid battle pass.
- **ChromaHunt** (URBISOFT) — direct minimalist-color competitor;
  brand-new (no ratings yet) but cannibalizing "chroma" search hits.
  Implication: don't rely on the bare word "chroma" as a unique key.

Heavy on theme, art, story. **Zero block-puzzle finalists. Zero

- **TR-49** — alt-history WWII codebreaking with nonlinear narrative
- **Is This Seat Taken?** — Saturday-morning-cartoon styled logic

- **Phozzle** (Indie App Spotlight) — puzzle that builds boards from

**Implication for CHROMA:** "Phase 1 chromatic-objective engine
ready, no campaign levels converted" is a personality that doesn't
show up in screenshots. We are leaving slot-1 pitch material on the
floor right now.

## The single move this week

**Convert + ship a 7-level "Chromatic: Chapter 1" campaign sequence
using the Phase 1 chromatic-objective engine, with a named theme and
chapter art.** Then calibrate the run-trace share cards specifically
for those 7 levels.

Reasoning:

1. Hungry Studio is not in color-mechanic space and is in pure
   optimization mode. Window is open, probably for one quarter.
2. The sort-puzzle wave proved single-mechanic puzzles can break the
   top-10 in a quarter if the mechanic is legible in a 6-second video.
   Chromatic-clear cascade is *more* visually arresting than arrow-sort.
3. Editorial favors story/theme/personality. Named chapters
   ("Chapter 1: Ignition") + minimal art convert "a feature" into
   "a thing" — what gets pitched in App-of-the-Day decks.
4. Run-trace share cards are inert without campaign content. Wordle
   works because every player shares the same daily result format.
   7 chapter levels give 7 shared share-targets across the first week.
5. "Chroma" is being cannibalized in search by ChromaHunt et al.
   Chapter names that own search terms beyond bare "chroma"
   ("Chromatic: Ignition") are now defensive infrastructure.

Build cost: engine ready, share-card pipeline shipped, return-habit
triad shipped. Missing: 7 hand-tuned levels + a chapter shell.
3-5 day job for one person.
