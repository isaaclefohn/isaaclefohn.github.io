CHROMA Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026 · Last updated: June 9, 2026 · Contact: kiwilefohn@gmail.com

1. About CHROMA

CHROMA ("the App") is a single-player block-puzzle game with optional leaderboards, daily challenges, in-app purchases, and ads. It is developed and operated by Isaac Lefohn, Oregon, USA, as a sole proprietor.

2. What we collect

Account / identity
  • Anonymous sign-in. When the App opens, it signs you in anonymously to our backend (Supabase). This creates a randomly generated user ID stored on your device and on our server. The ID is not linked to your name, email, phone, or any real-world identity.
  • Optional display name (you choose it) for leaderboards. If set, it is associated with the anonymous ID and visible to other players on leaderboards.
Gameplay and app usage
  • Scores, levels reached, streaks, achievements — stored on your device and synced to our backend for leaderboard and stats display.
  • Anonymized event analytics via PostHog (US region) — events like opening a screen, starting/winning a level, watching an ad, claiming a reward. No board state or in-game choices are sent.
Purchases
  • If you buy in-app purchases, Apple processes the payment — we never see your payment method. Apple sends us a purchase record to verify server-side. We store the product ID, the transaction timestamp, and an entitlement flag (for example, "ad-free active") tied to your anonymous user ID.
Ads
  • The App may show ads via Google AdMob. In the current version, all ads are non-personalized: the App does not request permission to track you, and does not provide your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to AdMob. AdMob may still process coarse technical data (such as IP address and device model) to serve, display, and measure ads.
Crash reports
  • Sentry receives anonymized crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, and a randomized session ID. No personal data.
What we do NOT collect
  • No name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, location, microphone audio, camera images, or health data.
  • We do not sell or rent any data to anyone.
  • We do not send any user data to AI providers or train AI models on it.

3. How we use what we collect

  • Run the leaderboards (display anonymous scores to other players).
  • Validate purchases so you receive what you paid for.
  • Detect and fix crashes.
  • Understand what features players use (for example, "how many people finish level 5?") so we can improve the game.
  • Serve ads that support development.

We do not use this data for advertising outside the App, sell it to third parties, or train AI models on it.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — backend database and auth
  • Upstash Redis — leaderboard storage
  • Vercel — API hosting
  • PostHog (US region) — analytics
  • Google AdMob — ads
  • Sentry — crash reporting
  • Apple — App Store and in-app purchases

5. Data retention

  • Local data lives on your device until you delete the App.
  • Backend data (anonymous user ID, scores, leaderboard entries, purchase records) is kept while the App operates, or up to 24 months after your last play session, after which it is anonymized or deleted.
  • Crash reports are retained for 90 days, then deleted.
  • Analytics events are retained per PostHog's default retention policy.

6. Your rights

  • Delete your data. Email kiwilefohn@gmail.com and include your leaderboard display name (if you set one); we will identify and delete the corresponding backend records within 30 days.
  • Opt out of analytics. Turn off "Anonymous Analytics" in the App's Settings screen — events stop immediately on your device. You can also email us to have your anonymous ID's past events excluded.
  • Personalized ads: the current version serves only non-personalized ads and never requests app-tracking permission, so there is nothing to opt out of. If a future version adds personalized ads, it will ask first via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • EU residents (GDPR): you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Articles 15-22). Email us to exercise any of these.
  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have rights of disclosure and deletion. We do not sell personal information, so no "Do Not Sell" mechanism is required.

7. Children

CHROMA is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The App requires no account, name, or contact information to play. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and provided data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add features, change vendors, or adapt to new regulations. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be announced in-App on the first launch after the change.
